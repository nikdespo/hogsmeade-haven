About Hogsmeade Haven

Hogsmeade Haven is an independent app developer creating focused, easy-to-use tools for personal growth and everyday life. Our goal is to build applications that are simple to understand, enjoyable to use, and useful in real-world routines.

Every application we create is designed with intention — thoughtful in its purpose, calm in its experience, and honest in what it offers.

Subliminal Images Guide

Create meaningful visual goals for your Subliminal Flash sessions. Use any AI image generator to bring your intentions to life. A personalized image can make your goal more familiar, recognizable, and emotionally meaningful to your mind, helping create a stronger visual connection with the intention behind your affirmation. Keep your image personal, vivid, and aligned with what you want to achieve.

How to create your images

Use any AI image generator you prefer:

ChatGPT Google Gemini Microsoft Copilot Midjourney Adobe Firefly
  1. Choose your goal

    Decide what you want the image to represent — health, fitness, wealth, travel, career, confidence, or any personal vision.

  2. Upload a photograph (optional)

    Provide a photo of yourself to the AI generator for a personalized visualization.

  3. Describe the visualization

    Tell the AI what you want the image to represent. Be specific about the scene, mood, and subject.

  4. Download the generated image

    Save the final image to your device.

  5. Add it to Subliminal Flash

    Import the image into the app and include it in your personalized session collection.

Image quality guidelines

Recommended
  • Clear, well-lit images
  • High resolution
  • Simple compositions
  • Recognizable subjects
  • Realistic visualizations
  • Strong goal connection
Avoid
  • Blurry or low-res images
  • Excessive text in image
  • Very complex scenes
  • Distracting elements
  • Unclear connection to goal
